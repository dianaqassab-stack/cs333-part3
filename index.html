How we cleaned the data

The file has 89,212 transactions (a 10% sample of Dubai Land Department records). Before building any chart we checked every column. These are the problems we found and what we did about each one. All views on this page use the same rules, so their numbers agree with each other.

Problem we foundWhat we didRows affected
Implausible or out-of-range dates: the earliest is 28 Nov 1967, and the file runs to 17 Sep 2026 (a partial month) Kept 1 Jan 2015 to 31 Aug 2026, the same window as Parts 1 and 2. Dates are read in UTC. 22,190 removed (21,664 before 2015, 526 after Aug 2026) → 67,022 kept
Mortgage and gift values are not market prices: median 7,804 AED/m² for mortgages and 11,476 for gifts, against 15,114 for sales Price views use Sales only. Mortgages and gifts are kept only for counting transactions. 51,339 sales in the window
Zero or extreme prices: one sale at 0 AED/m² (a land plot recorded at 14 AED in total), values as low as 2.5 AED/m², and buildings up to 414,000 AED/m² Price views keep sales between 1,000 and 100,000 AED/m². Out of 46,286 unit and villa sales, only 75 fall outside that range. 237 sales removed
Impossible sizes: 116 units under 15 m², including a "1 B/R" of 0.83 m² and many 8 m² "studios" Price views keep properties of at least 15 m². A real studio is about 25 m² or more. 122 sales removed (both rules together: 356, 0.7% of sales)
Missing number of rooms (12,766 rows in the window): mostly land plots (6,934), but also 4,112 villas, 869 units and 851 buildings Shown as "Not stated" instead of dropped, so we don't lose those sales. kept, relabelled
Missing nearest metro station (24,990 rows in the window), and spelling mistakes ("Buj Khalifa…", "Jumeirah Beach Resdency", "Palm Deira Metro Stations") Spellings fixed. Rows with no station are labelled "No station listed" and left out of the station views only. Note that the column also lists Dubai Tram stops (for example Harbour Tower and Mina Seyahi), not only metro stations. 5,802 spellings fixed; 24,990 with no station
One property usage written in Arabic ("أخرى", which means "Other") Merged into "Other". 293 rows
Two communities without coordinates (Al Maha, Al Layan1) Kept. They only drop out of point maps. 2 rows
Missing days: 1,545 of 4,261 days have no transactions. Most are weekends, but 327 are weekdays, mostly in 2015–2019 when volumes were low (this is a 10% sample) Nothing removed. Time views group by month (or longer) so that empty days don't create false drops. none
Medians based on very few sales are unreliable (the same finding as Part 2) Any group (community, station, room type) with fewer than 10 sales is greyed out or hidden in price views. depends on the view

Derived fields we added: year, is_offplan (true or false), size_band (under 50, 50–100, 100–200, 200–500, 500+ m²), price_m (total price in millions of AED), rooms_clean, metro_clean and usage_clean. We checked that meter_sale_price always equals actual_worth ÷ procedure_area (true for every row), so it is safe to use directly as the price per m².
